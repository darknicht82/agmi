# Evidence rules

- Every important claim needs provenance: `claim → evidence → source`.
- Store canonical URL, capture timestamp and content hash.
- Preserve an excerpt/locator sufficient for human review.
- Distinguish observation, inference and hypothesis.
- LLM-generated confidence is not proof.
- Contradictory evidence must remain visible.

Ningún `Problem` pasa a prioridad comercial sin referencias a `Evidence`.
El score nunca compensa ausencia de evidencia.
Un número fabricado no es línea base.
